Projects

Energy imports and renewable energy: A multi-country analysis

This report analyzes the relationship between how much energy a country imports and how much renewable energy it employs as a share of total production. The data analysis was conducted using World Bankd data, R, and RStudio. This was my final project for the course Green and Digital Transformation in the Goethe Universität Master in International Economics and Economic Policy program.

BlightWatch NOLA

BlightWatch NOLA is a data web app that displays blighted, or distressed, property data in the city of New Orleans, Louisiana.

The Most Famous Musicians You've Never Heard of

This project aims to identify popular artists outside of the Anglospehere, and understand global music trends more broadly. Using data scraped from a variety of sources, including Spotify chart data and public opinion data, Sarah Helmbrecht and I created this Jupyter notebook data science project that includes ETL, visualisations, and an implementation of a random forest machine learning model.
